# IDEMIA enquiry (Task 2)

## What IDEMIA is likely to be able to offer, realistically

* IDEMIA's payments business (IDEMIA Secure Transactions) makes cards and wearable form factors for
  **issuers**. Its wearable page describes a SIM-size "mini FOB" for wristbands, keepers and key rings,
  certified for Visa and Mastercard ([IDEMIA wearables](https://www.idemia.com/wearable-payment-means)).
  Public references are issuer programmes: UAE Exchange in 2017 and Westpac PayWear.
* The UK office is listed in Tewkesbury ([Craft](https://craft.co/idemia-uk)).
* **What they almost certainly cannot do:** sell one *live*, personalised payment device to a private
  individual. Live personalisation is done under contract for an issuer, with that issuer's keys.
  An engineering sample would carry **test credentials**, which genuine UK terminals decline.
* **What they might usefully do:** confirm whether the Flybuy Mini FOB or a successor is current, share
  *public* dimensions and antenna information, and name UK issuers or TSPs that offer consumer-activatable
  wearables. A contact might also know whether any UK bank is piloting tags like the Cash App Wand.

## Conflict-of-interest and etiquette checklist (do this before sending)

- [ ] Send from your **personal** email address. Use no employer signature, title or domain.
- [ ] Check your employer's policy on contact with vendors and suppliers, and on gifts or samples. If IDEMIA
      is a supplier to your employer, consider telling your manager or compliance team first.
- [ ] Ask only for **public or non-confidential** information. Don't ask for datasheets under NDA,
      keys, personalisation scripts or certification documents.
- [ ] Offer to pay normal prices. Don't ask for free goods.
