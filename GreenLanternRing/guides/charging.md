# Charging guide

1. **Take the ring off.** It only fits the cradle when it's off your finger, by design.
2. Slide it onto the cradle's mandrel with the emblem towards the back, so the head drops into its
   pocket. The contacts are on the head's side face, against the two spring pins.
3. Plug a USB-C cable into the cradle (any 5 V USB charger).
4. The charge LED is on while charging and goes off when it's done (about 2.5 h from empty). You can
   leave it overnight: the MCP73831 stops at full charge. **Confirm this on your unit once (test
   CHG-02) before relying on it.**

Safety rules:
* Charge on a hard, non-flammable surface, not on a bed or sofa.
* If the ring feels warm (above body temperature) while charging, unplug it and log it as CHG-04.
* If the head bulges or the bezel lifts, the cell may be swelling. Stop using it, open it outdoors, and
  take the cell to a battery recycling point. Never bin a lithium cell.
* The two contacts are protected by a diode, so touching them with keys can't short the battery. Still,
  keep them clean and dry.
* Storage: charge to about 50–80% and switch off. Top up monthly.
